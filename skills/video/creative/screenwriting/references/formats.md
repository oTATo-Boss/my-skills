# Script formats by task

Adapt headings to the existing project. Stable scene/section IDs matter more than a particular document extension.

## Dramatic scene

```text
SCENE S03 - INT. WORKSHOP - NIGHT
Story time: Day 2, after S02. Estimated duration: 45-65 seconds; untested.
Present: MAYA, JIN. Object state: brass key with Maya, dry coat on chair.
Purpose: Maya chooses to reveal the key rather than protect her alibi.

Maya places a locked tin between them. Jin reaches for it. She keeps one hand
on the lid, then takes the key from her pocket and sets it beside his hand.

JIN
You said you never came back.

MAYA
I said nobody saw me.

Exit state: Jin knows Maya returned; he holds the key. The tin remains closed.
Production note: Preserve key handoff across coverage. No interior monologue.
```

The action gives actors and image makers an observable event. A shot list can later decide close-ups and coverage. A screenplay need not narrate lens choices for every line.

## Explainer/documentary audiovisual section

| ID/time estimate | Narration or dialogue | Picture/action | Fact/source | Sound/transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X02 / 8-15 s | A shorter pendulum swings faster under these assumptions. | Two pendulums, equal release angle; visibly different periods. | Source F02; small-angle model, idealized illustration | A/B tick pattern; compare before changing variables |

Record exact source pointers in the project's research ledger. Label simulation/model assumptions on screen or in accompanying notes when omission would mislead. Do not use a simulation as observational proof or put a synthetic quotation in a real interview subject's mouth.

## Series planning

```yaml
episode: E02
entry_state: Maya suspects the workshop was searched; Jin knows nothing of the key.
question: Will she trade her alibi for his trust?
changes:
  - S03: She gives him the key; he learns she returned.
  - S04: They discover the tin is empty; their shared plan must change.
resolved: Whether Maya admits returning.
carried_forward: Who emptied the tin; why the hinge is recently repaired.
exit_state: Both know the tin is empty; Jin retains the key.
```

For short episodes, choose a compact movement; for longer episodes, develop intermediate turns and secondary strands. Series planning can remain at an outline stage until scripts are requested. A season bible should distinguish fixed canon from provisional ideas.

## Music-led writing

Map actual timecodes for intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge and outro only when present in the track. Assign a visual/motional development to each section. Note sync anchors where an edit, action or lyric matters; other cuts can breathe across beats. A narrative MV additionally uses scene entry/exit states. Exact track duration and edit points come from the supplied audio, not a generic song template.
