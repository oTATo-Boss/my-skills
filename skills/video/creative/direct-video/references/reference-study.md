# Reference analysis

Choose references for purpose, audience and constraints. Include a quality benchmark and a contrasting technique when useful. Luxury, documentary, comedy and native-social work have different criteria.

Prefer original creator/studio pages, official training and source repositories. Social posts are valuable discovery paths, not automatically verified tutorials. If access is blocked, record the gap and switch to an accessible primary source; do not claim to have watched the blocked clip.

## Evidence labels
- text-read: actual explanation/prompt read; no visual claim.
- stills-viewed: specific frames inspected; no timing/audio claim.
- sequence-sampled: timestamped sequences inspected; conclusions bounded by sampling interval.
- played: actual playback observed with a supported tool.
- audio-heard: actually heard; waveform analysis does not qualify.
- executed: ran identified source/tool on identified inputs.
- discovered-only: listing/search result; no detailed evidence.

Record author, URL, accessed date, evidence level, timestamps, mechanism, inference and unknowns. A website background loop can differ from the full film; verify duration and framing.

## Study card
1. Intended viewer response.
2. Attention at opening, peak and ending.
3. Subject position/scale, empty space, value contrast, type/image relationship.
4. Anticipation/action/settle/hold, trajectory, acceleration, deformation and camera.
5. Shot lengths, continuity anchors, reveal order and meaning of transitions.
6. Sound only if actually heard; otherwise name the unresolved audio question.
7. One reusable principle and one context where it is inappropriate.
8. A small original experiment isolating the principle; change identity/content and do not repackage reference assets.

Translate observation → principle → project decision → evidence needed.
Example: fixed title while foreground disappears → persistent anchor provides continuity → hold one title across a cut → inspect drift/overlap at that boundary.

Do not copy reference media into the reusable skill. Persist original observations and source links; keep research media in the task. Prefer a few understood mechanisms to an unexamined bookmark collection.
