The exchange underneath

Everything a serious
desk expects.

Cross-margin

One collateral pool backs every position. Capital works across markets instead of sitting idle in silos.

Up to 50× leverage

Granular leverage per market with transparent, on-chain margin and liquidation logic you can audit.

Sub-ms matching

A purpose-built engine clears orders at centralized-exchange speed, then commits the result on-chain.

Fully on-chain book

Bids, asks, and fills are all settled state — no trusted sequencer, no off-chain order database.

MEV-resistant

Deterministic price-time priority leaves no room to reorder, sandwich, or front-run your flow.

API & agent SDK

Typed SDK, REST, and streaming WebSockets — the same surface the terminal uses, open to every builder.
