## Observations from sampled references

### UI morph — 14 seconds, 720×720 research copy
Text-read + sequence-sampled; audio stream present but not heard.
- 0.000s starts with a small centered button; 1.861s expands to a music-player container; 4.653s becomes a slider; 5.584s is a toggle; 6.515s becomes tabs; 8.376s is a chart; 10.237s is a command palette; 12.099s resolves to a toast; 13.029s returns to the button.
- Stable visual center, restrained color and cursor action supply coherence across large size/function changes. The chart fills more space because it carries more information.
- Dense samples 4.917–5.083s show the elongated slider contracting; this is bounded evidence of deformation, not proof of the full clip's smoothness.
- Transfer: connect states through persistent geometry and a visible cause. Do not impose this UI motif on emotional live-action or documentary work.

### Procreate Dreams website preview — 9.643 seconds, 720×720, no audio stream
Stills-viewed + sequence-sampled. The project page lists a 17-second film; this preview is not that full film.
- 0.873s concentrates colored blocks around the character; 2.619s uses large near-camera feet/blocks and a receding body; 3.492s gives the reaching hand and pencil the frame; 4.365s opens significant empty space around a much smaller figure; 5.238s encloses it in a black spiral; 6.111s jumps to an eye close-up.
- Scale and framing changes carry emphasis; flat neutral ground makes saturated accents and silhouette easy to read. Hand-painted edges add character without photorealistic texture.
- Transfer: plan shot-scale contrast and motivated foreground shapes. This does not imply constant fast motion, extreme perspective or saturated colors for every project.
