# 正常人体比例3D动画预设

- **标识**：`normal-human-proportions-3d-animation`
- **预设版本**：`1.0.0`
- **适用模式**：通用3D（`cg`）
- **适用模板**：`portrait`、`head-four`、`half`、`body-three`、`board`
- **控制范围**：自然人体结构与比例、动画化面部建模、皮肤与眼睛表现、立体发束及整体3D渲染。

## 风格提示词

```text
Create a fully rendered 3D animated human character with natural human anatomy and normal, age-appropriate head-to-body proportions. Preserve the individual character's specified age, gender, ethnicity, face silhouette, body type, skin tone and personality. Keep plausible limb lengths, joints, hands and feet; use controlled animation stylization rather than chibi proportions, oversized toy heads, an extremely tiny waist or excessively elongated legs. Do not impose one fixed head-to-body ratio on every age or body type.

Use clearly authored 3D facial sculpting: clean, intentional cheek, jaw, nose and lip volumes; expressive eyes and brows; readable eyelid shapes and coherent facial proportions. Gently simplify and shape the facial planes while retaining the character's individual identity. Detail should come primarily from modeled forms and art-directed shading. Keep subtle stylization readable as an animated character rather than a photographic human with softened skin.

Skin has a smooth, subtly varied animation shader, soft restrained subsurface scattering, clean broad color gradients and controlled highlights, with very restrained surface microtexture. Avoid photographic human skin, dense pores, peach fuzz, photoreal digital doubles and AAA realistic game-head materials. Keep the surface dimensional and softly responsive to light rather than waxy or uniformly plastic.

Eyes have designed irises, clear eyelid support and controlled readable highlights integrated with the stylized face. Allow expressive animation shaping without oversized childlike toy eyes. Build hair from coherent, dimensional sculpted locks and layered strands, with readable flow, volume and controlled highlights. The hairstyle, hair length and hair color come from the separate character design, not from this style preset.

Clothing and accessories have dimensional geometry, readable construction and distinct material responses within the same animated rendering language. Preserve any separately specified product fidelity or realistic material requirements without making the character's face photorealistic.

Deliver a visibly modeled, fully rendered, commercial-quality 3D animated character. Avoid flat 2D artwork, cel-shaded substitutes, low-detail toy forms and photographic digital-human rendering. Use the background, lighting, camera, framing, view count and layout required by the selected character template. The preset controls modeling and rendering only; character identity, clothing, hairstyle, pose and scene come from the current task. Do not import a particular film's protagonists, costumes, props, lighting or setting.
```

## 使用边界

“正常人体比例”指自然、协调的人类结构与头身关系；面部仍有明确的动画化设计，不要求照相级五官或皮肤。年龄感、具体脸型及体型按角色方案保持，不固定为年轻女性或某种族裔。已有角色身份按其确认资料继承。

本预设不包含人物设定、发型/发色、服装、品牌产品、背景、固定灯光、渲染器或宫格排版；这些由本次任务与原模板提供。用户未要求产品写实时，不自动为所有配饰添加写实例外。
