# Lighting and Optics Rules (Friend-Camera Physics)

Both professional-photographer gimmicks and creamy DSLR bokeh immediately trigger the "AI-generated" feel. Keep optical physics strictly grounded in what a real friend captures on a phone or compact digital camera.

## Default Lens and Depth of Field

- Always default to a **24mm-28mm smartphone main lens** or a **2000s compact CCD / point-and-shoot digital camera**.
- Maintain **natural small-sensor deep depth of field** so backgrounds, storefront signs, furniture, cables, and foreground props remain naturally readable rather than melting into creamy blur.

## The 3 Flattering Real-World Lighting Modes

- **Direct Compact/Phone Flash (Indoor or Night)**
  - Crisp direct on-camera flash mixed with warm ambient lamps, lounge sconces, arcade/KTV lights, or blue-hour twilight.
  - Produces bright eye catchlights, radiant dewy skin, subtle vintage highlight bloom, and realistic hard shadow falloff behind the subject.
- **Clean Daytime Natural Light**
  - Bright outdoor afternoon sun or window/open-shopfront daylight with crisp, honest shadows and true-to-life color.
  - Never fake movie-poster golden-hour rim light.
- **Warm Interior Spotlights**
  - Boutique fitting-room recessed spotlights or warm bedroom vanity mirror bulbs casting believable shadows and slight mirror reflections.

## Strictly Forbidden "Too-Photography / AI" Triggers

- **Never use** f/1.4 creamy DSLR bokeh or studio portrait separation.
- **Never use** slow-shutter neon drag / motion-blur light trails.
- **Never use** dramatic Venetian-blind chiaroscuro light slicing.
- **Never use** extreme 0.5x (14mm) ultra-wide architectural distortion.
- **Never use** harsh unflattering convenience-store fluorescent tubes or dreary grey overcast lighting that drains color and charm from the subject.
- **Never use** quality buzzwords ("8k", "masterpiece", "cinematic", "editorial photography", "atmospheric lighting").
