# Single-Vector Tension Playbooks and Event Pool

Because the camera stays simple and real, the jump from 75 points ("safe pretty") to 95 points ("instant save") comes from pushing **one clear emotional vector on the subject to 10/10** inside a clean, tasteful, lived-in space.

## 1. The 4 Extreme Single-Vector Playbooks

### Pure-Desire / High-Allure Vector

- **Camera & Proximity**: Close, intimate slightly high-angle framing.
- **Body Tension**: Arched waist-to-hip curve or leaning over a dark velvet sofa armrest / open night car window; one thin strap slipping casually off a glistening bare shoulder; fingertips brushing lightly near the collarbone.
- **Expression**: Heavy-lidded, breathless, magnetic gaze straight into the lens with slightly parted glossy lips (zero polite smile).
- **Styling & Makeup**: Luminous dewy skin with subtle collarbone/shoulder shimmer, softly smudged brown eyeliner, plump glossy rose-berry lips, draped satin corset or sleek black halter with a thin sparkling clavicle chain.

### Ultra-Cute Vector

- **Camera & Proximity**: Close playful high-angle framing looking down into big sparkling eyes.
- **Body & Micro-Gesture**: Cheeky soft pout with one fingertip poking a rosy cheek, oversized fluffy paw-sleeves covering half the hands, leaning eagerly toward the lens.
- **Hair & Makeup**: Fluffy low twin pigtails with oversized cream velvet ribbons or cute clips; prominent rosy strawberry under-eye igari blush across nose bridge, sparkling aegyo-sal shimmer, ultra-glossy strawberry jelly lips.
- **Styling**: Fluffy pastel mohair sweater slipped off one shoulder over a white lace cami bow, pearl-and-heart choker, plaid pleated mini skirt.

### High-Glamour / Heiress Vector

- **Camera & Proximity**: Three-quarter back-and-waist silhouette turning over a bare shoulder toward a compact flash camera.
- **Expression**: Cool, poised, devastatingly confident siren gaze with lacquered deep-berry slightly parted lips.
- **Styling & Makeup**: Sculpted glossy waves swept over one shoulder, structured black velvet or champagne satin corset mini dress with sparkling crystal straps, crystal-and-pearl drop earrings and tennis clavicle necklace catching micro-flash sparkles.

### Fresh / Sporty-Breezy Vector

- **Camera & Proximity**: Playful slightly high 24mm wide smartphone angle elongating posture on a sunlit bench or boat deck.
- **Expression**: Bright, carefree, sparkling smile with windblown hair strands.
- **Styling**: High ponytail with white satin ribbon or tortoiseshell sunglasses pushed on head, fitted white halter athletic top + pleated tennis skirt + sneakers, or white eyelet-lace tie-front halter + frayed denim shorts.

## 2. Environment Sweet Spot (Neither Shabby Nor Sterile)

- **Never shabby**: Avoid rusty water tanks, cracked dirty concrete, dilapidated rooftops, or dreary grey parking lots.
- **Never sterile hotel/Airbnb catalog**: Avoid empty all-white bedrooms (white wall + white bed + white curtain + one staged open book and empty mug).
- **Never overtrained AI stock clichés**: Avoid pristine untouched croissants beside neatly wrapped Parisian rose bouquets.
- **Target clean, tasteful, genuinely lived-in spaces with 3-4 coherent micro-imperfections**: Half-eaten dessert, condensation droplets, crumpled napkin, receipt, clothes on fitting-room hangers, handbag on stool, handwritten price cards in flower buckets, tangled cords on a vanity desk, or slight mirror smudges.

## 3. Diverse Non-Dining Event Pool (Rotate Actively)

- **Shopping & Errands**: Boutique fitting-room mirror try-on, independent vinyl/select shop browsing, picking unwrapped tulips at a neighborhood flower shop.
- **Nightlife & Social**: Dimly lit marble-and-brass cocktail lounge, dark velvet lounge booth, KTV booth with wireless mic and tambourine, Korean self-photo booth (curtain + preview screen + prop shelf), late-night arcade claw machines.
- **Outdoors & Travel**: Sunlit outdoor tennis court bench, white leisure boat/yacht deck, modern riverside glass-railing terrace at blue hour, dark-leather car passenger seat with window rolled down along a glowing night river.
- **Cozy & Personal**: Neighborhood cat cafe wooden floor with Ragdoll cats, warm-lit cluttered vanity mirror touch-up before going out, retro CCD lived-in bedroom night hangout.
- **Dining (use selectively)**: Late-night Korean dessert cafe booth or cozy izakaya skewers with real table clutter.
