# Portrait Art Direction

Turn portrait and lifestyle image requests from generic AI templates into authentic, 90+ point Xiaohongshu-grade candid photographs. Every frame must stay strictly inside the golden sweet spot: ordinary friend-camera optics and genuine lived-in spaces (never "try-hard photography" tricks that scream AI) paired with maximum single-vector charm, posture tension, and high-effort styling on the subject (never safe, polite, 75-point posing).

## When to Use

- Designing single portraits or 3-shot lifestyle/fashion photo sets for a character or subject.
- Styling hair, makeup, outfits, and accessories around Xiaohongshu-style candid realism, retro CCD/point-and-shoot flash, or crisp daytime smartphone photography.
- Fixing flat, stock-photo, over-photographed, sterile, or "immediately AI-looking" results while pushing charm and beauty to 90+ points.

Match the deliverable to the request. For art direction or prompt-writing tasks, deliver text. Generate images only when the user requests generation, following their tool and reference-image requirements.

## Core Workflow

1. **Pick One Clear Emotional Vector (10/10, Never Safe 75-Point Posing):** Choose Pure-Desire, Ultra-Cute, High-Glamour, or Sporty-Breezy from [Vectors and events](references/vectors-and-events.md). Commit fully through camera proximity, posture tension, micro-expression, and outfit cut.
2. **Choose a Clean, Tasteful, Lived-In Space:** Avoid both shabby/run-down locations and sterile all-white hotel rooms. Rotate away from cafes and dining unless specifically requested.
3. **Lock Friend-Camera Optics:** Apply [Lighting and optics](references/lighting-and-optics.md) (24mm-28mm phone or compact CCD deep depth of field, plus 1 of the 3 flattering real-world lighting modes). Never use slow-shutter neon drag, Venetian-blind light slicing, extreme 0.5x architectural distortion, or f/1.4 creamy DSLR bokeh.
4. **Build Cohesive Multi-Shot Rhythm:** For 3-shot sets, keep character, outfit, location, and lighting locked across all frames. Vary strictly through Shot 1 (establishing three-quarter/medium), Shot 2 (angled posture or prop interaction), and Shot 3 (tight close-up or mirror/prop moment).
5. **Write Concrete Natural-Language Prompts:** Describe exact lens depth, light source, fabric textures, 1-2 restrained jewelry pieces, and 3-4 coherent lived-in objects. Never use quality buzzwords ("8k", "masterpiece", "cinematic", "editorial photography").
