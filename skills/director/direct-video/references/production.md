## Precedence and limitations
- User/project direction takes priority over optional workflow defaults. Keep engine contracts separate: HyperFrames owns its media; Remotion owns frame evaluation.
- A template's palette, duration, three alternatives, camera motion or fixed easing is scoped to that template, not global taste.
- Delegated artistic judgment does not fabricate approval for protected source assets, publication folders, external jobs or spending.
- Installed skill files do not prove a tool works. Inspect dependencies and do a minimal test before promising execution.
- The installed device-canvas skill references additional skill names that may be absent; resolve them only when needed and report missing dependencies. Do not pretend they were loaded.
- The title-takeover generator can download local segmentation weights on first use. Evaluate the need before running it; preserve the original presenter take.

## Project and cost control
Keep brief, design contract, shot/beat list, assets/provenance, source and QA together. A simple piece can use one compact document. Shot record: time | purpose | composition | action | camera | text/read window | sound | transition.

Rough out complete rhythm before expensive detail. Estimate cost using a representative difficult section. Recheck the final encoded assembly.

Use existing skills first. For a material gap search original creators, official docs and free Skillry entries. Follow each install.md and official CLI; inspect code before executing. Never enroll in paid services without authorization.

Refresh APIs, provider controls, platform overlays/specs and licenses when needed. Do not re-research stable design principles for every minor edit.
